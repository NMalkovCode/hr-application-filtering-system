Senior Data Analyst — Northwind Logistics (Leeds, hybrid)

About the role
Northwind Logistics is looking for a Senior Data Analyst to join our young, energetic Insights team.
You will own reporting for our UK warehouse network, build dashboards used by operations leaders,
and run analyses that shape how we plan capacity and staffing.

What you'll do
- Build and maintain Power BI dashboards for warehouse and transport KPIs
- Write complex SQL against our Snowflake data warehouse
- Partner with operations managers to define metrics and present findings to senior leadership
- Design and evaluate A/B tests on picking and routing processes
- Mentor two junior analysts

Requirements
- At least 5 years' experience in a data analyst or BI role
- Advanced SQL is essential
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau
- Degree in a quantitative subject (Maths, Statistics, Economics, Computer Science or similar)
- Native English speaker
- Must have the right to work in the UK

Nice to have
- Python (pandas) for analysis and automation
- Experience in logistics, supply chain or e-commerce
- Knowledge of statistics and experiment design
- Snowflake or dbt experience

Other
- Hybrid: 2 days per week in our Leeds office
- Occasional travel to warehouse sites across the UK
- Willing to work occasional weekends during peak season
